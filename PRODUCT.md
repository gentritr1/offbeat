# OFFBEAT

## Register
brand

## Users
Design-conscious music listeners and portfolio reviewers who want to explore a tactile audio product and creative interaction design.

## Product Purpose
An OFFBEAT speaker concept, explicitly selected by the user. Make it tangible through 3D manipulation, finish selection, an exploded view, and a browser sound studio. This is a fictional portfolio concept rather than a commercial store.

## Brand Personality
Tactile, rhythmic, irreverent. Delight through physical controls and confident, clear language.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS cards, default purple gradients, excessive scrolling effects, ornamental statistics, and fake reviews.

## Design Principles
Let the product be the spectacle. Make exploration immediate. Reward curiosity with working interactions. Keep typography, materials, and movement coherent. Fit the experience to the device.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard controls, readable contrast, visible focus, reduced motion, touch controls, no automatic audio, graceful WebGL fallback.
